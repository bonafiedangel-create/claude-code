# Prompt review: Master Video Director v1

The core of v1 is strong. The conversion psychology engine, the stacking rule, the ethics rule, the shot card and the edit passes are better than most paid prompt packs. Keep them.

The problems are about fit and load. v1 was written for a generic "corporate to creator" woman, but your story is different. It also asks a solo founder to run a film studio's paperwork. Every fix below is already applied in `02-master-video-director-v2.md`.

## Must fix

**1. It tells the wrong story.**
Mode 4, The Letter ("to the woman still in the cubicle"), The Last Day and the belonging example all assume someone leaving a corporate office. Your arc is a multi-chapter reinvention: choir at 15, then fashion, flowers and weddings, then government, then e-commerce, then the 2020 freeze, then relearning everything. Corporate escape is only one chapter of it.
*Fix:* mode 4 becomes **Start over in style**. The formats now use your arc, and the audience is defined by your situation, not by gender or job title.

**2. It has no page strategy.**
v1 says "one Project per brand" but never explains what a founder page is for. You told me what it's for: you are the niche, followers follow the journey, and the brands come in at 50k.
*Fix:* a new **Page Lock** sets the goal, the audience, 3 content pillars and a **Hub rule**. Before 50k, your brands appear only as chapters of your story, never as ads. After 50k, each brand gets a handoff episode.

**3. It ignores your 25k dormant followers.**
That audience is the most dramatic fact in your story. It's also a practical question: if the founder page is the 2018 account, the first videos have to win those people back.
*Fix:* a "Return" episode type, plus an open question in the locks file.

**4. It's too heavy for one person.**
v1 has 10 modes, 13 commands, a 10-part dossier and three choices at every checkpoint. That's how a project stalls at week three.
*Fix:* Quick mode is the default and the Full Dossier is only for flagship videos. The menu is cut to 7 modes. Checkpoints happen only at hook, script and edit.

**5. The taste gate can block everything.**
"No video until the Bible exists" stops you dead if you only have one reference.
*Fix:* the look can be **provisional**. It's built from whatever you have and labelled provisional until real references lock it.

**6. The prompt breaks its own honesty rule.**
- It says "In the Claude app you can trim, join, reframe… mix voice over music in the workspace." That depends on which tools are switched on and isn't guaranteed.
- The weekly scheduled scout depends on your plan.
- The GitHub repo list is dated, and versions go stale.

*Fix:* all three are now conditional. The model has to check before promising, and the repo list is marked as a dated reference.

**7. AI-cast voice conflicts with "follow my journey."**
On a page where you are the product, a synthetic narrator weakens trust.
*Fix:* your own voice is the default for published videos. AI voices are allowed only as a temporary track for timing drafts, and never published as you.

**8. The government chapter carries legal and privacy risk.**
"People got fired unjustly" is a strong story beat, but it could identify and accuse real people.
*Fix:* a new rule bans names, institutions, parties, dates or details that identify the office or colleagues. Tell it as *your* experience ("the climate changed, so I left"), never as an accusation.

## Should fix

**9. Being multilingual isn't used.** It's now a signature. English carries every video, and one French (or Creole, Spanish or Italian) line becomes the recurring sign-off. That uses the Familiarity mechanism.

**10. There's no definition of a win.** Growing to 50k needs a scoreboard, and raw views is the wrong one. v2 tracks follows, sends and saves per post, compared with your own median.

**11. There's no capacity plan.** v2 adds a weekly rhythm: one batch day, 3 posts a week to start, and the series counter.

**12. Some numbers were stated as fact.** v1 says "Default cut length 0.8–2.0s" and "2.3–2.8 wps." Those are fine as starting defaults, so v2 labels them as defaults to test against your own results.

## Kept as is

Prime directive · operating rules 2–9 · the mechanism table · stacking rule · CTA-to-goal map · ethics rule · hook formula · the shot card · coverage plan · character lock · edit passes and quality gate · the scout's "outliers, not raw views" rule · the Playbook learning loop.
