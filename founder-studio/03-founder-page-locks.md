## Face policy: my recommendation

**"Face on the turn."** On a page where you are the niche, viewers follow a person they recognise, and the Familiarity mechanism needs a face. You don't have to be a talking head every time, though.

- **Default:** partial. Hands making things, walking from behind, a silhouette at a window, reflections.
- **Full face:** on the emotional turn of each video, and in direct-to-camera episodes (Intro / Return, and every few series episodes).
- **Starting rule to test:** at least 1 post in 3 has a direct-to-camera moment. Adjust based on your follows per post.

## Voice Lock: my recommendation

- **Voice:** your own. "You handle it" means I direct it rather than replace it. An AI narrator on a follow-my-journey page reads as fake.
- **Archetype:** intimate confidante with a dry, amused edge. Warm, unhurried, never salesy.
- **Pace:** slower than conversational on the emotional beats. Hold a 0.4–0.6s pause before the turn line.
- **Recording:** phone 10–15 cm from your mouth, in a closet or under a blanket, plus 10 seconds of room tone.
- **Signature sign-off:** one short French line, e.g. *"On recommence. Avec style."* ("We start again. In style.") You can swap this for your own words.
